Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

iopix is a client for a photo server you run yourself. Your photos stay between your devices and your own server. The developer of iopix collects nothing.

The iopix apps

The iopix apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV connect to an iopix server that you, or someone you trust, runs.

  • The apps send your photos, your account sign-in and your edits only to the iopix server whose address you enter, and to nobody else.
  • The developer of iopix collects no personal data, uses no analytics, tracking or advertising, and cannot see your photos or your server.
  • Location search uses Apple's own Maps service, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Photos you choose to upload from your photo library are sent to your iopix server and nowhere else. The apps only read the photos you pick.
  • Your server address and sign-in are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

The iopix server

The iopix server runs on your own computer and keeps your library, accounts and settings there. It does not send your photos or personal data to the developer. It only contacts outside services you can turn on or off:

  • downloading AI models, once, when you install a feature (the AI analysis itself runs on your server);
  • a daily check for a newer version, which fetches the public list of versions and sends nothing about your install;
  • place search on OpenStreetMap, which sends only the text you type;
  • Google Maps, Street View and Cloud Vision, only if you add your own Google key, and then under Google's terms.

Whoever runs an iopix server is responsible for the data on it. If you use a server someone else runs, ask them how they look after it.

Children

iopix collects no data from anyone, children included.
