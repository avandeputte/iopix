# Dates and locations

## Date and time

In the info panel click **change** next to the date, or use **Edit** for a selection. Set a date, or **shift** every selected photo by the same amount (handy when a camera's clock was wrong). iopix writes the EXIF, IPTC and XMP date fields digiKam writes, and digiKam's own date.

## Location

**Set location** in the info panel, or in the selection bar for many photos at once:

- **search** a place, landmark or address,
- **click** the map or **drag** the pin,
- **paste** coordinates ("50.85, 4.35"),
- **copy** from the previous photo,
- or **remove** the location.

The altitude fills in with the ground height at that point unless you type one.

**Suggest location** lists where the photos taken nearest in time were taken, within six hours, with how long before or after. With Google Cloud Vision set up, it can also ask Google to recognise a landmark in the photo.

## Placing photos on the map

**Map › Place photos** shows a tray of photos without a location (your current search and filters apply). Find a place, select photos (Shift for a range), and click the map, or drag them onto it. While placing, single markers can be dragged to a better spot. Each change has Undo in its message.

## GPS tracks

**Edit › Correlate with GPS track** matches photos to a GPX file from a phone or GPS logger, like digiKam's geolocation editor: positions are interpolated between track points, with the camera's time zone, a clock correction and a maximum gap. You see a preview before anything is written.

## Satellite and Street View

Every map has a **Map / Satellite** switch. **Street View** is offered when setting a location and in the info panel.
