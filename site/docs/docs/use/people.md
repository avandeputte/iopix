# People and faces

**People** lists everyone named in your library, from digiKam's face regions, each with a face crop as their picture.

![People](/img/people.jpg){.shot}

## Sorting and groups

Sort people **by group**, **by family name**, **by name** or by **most photos**. Groups (family, friends, a team) let you keep the list tidy; editors can arrange them.

A person's page shows all their photos, and **Then and now** for that person.

## Faces still to name

**People › Faces** lists faces nobody has named yet:

- **Unnamed faces** that digiKam or iopix found,
- **Suggestions**, where a name was proposed but not confirmed,
- **Ignored** faces.

With the face-grouping model installed, unnamed faces are shown in **look-alike groups**, each with a "looks like" guess. Name a whole group in one go, or ignore it. A guess is never written anywhere until you accept it.

See [Naming faces](../edit/faces.md) for the editing side.

## Finding faces nothing has marked

The face-detection model can look for faces in photos where none is marked yet: one photo (info panel › People › detect faces), one folder, or the whole library. Faces it finds are added as unnamed faces, the way digiKam adds them. A face that overlaps an existing one is never added twice.

## Accounts and people

An admin can link each account to a person. That person's face then becomes the account's picture on the sign-in screen and in the menu.
