# Photos and the timeline

**Photos** is the whole library as one long timeline, newest first, grouped by day. It stays smooth with hundreds of thousands of photos, because only what is on screen is drawn.

![Photos](/img/photos.jpg){.shot}

## Getting around

- The **timeline rail** on the right shows how many photos each month has. Hover it for a preview of that month; drag it to jump.
- A **date box** in the bar above the grid jumps straight to a day.
- Choose from **three tile sizes**.
- **Sort** in several orders, including oldest first and, once sharpness has been measured, sharpest first.

## Badges

Thumbnails can carry small badges: faces, a place, favorite. Turn them on or off in **Settings › Display › Thumbnail badges**.

## Videos

Videos play in the viewer, with a thumbnail made from the video itself. Formats a browser cannot play directly are converted for playback.

## Light and dark

**Settings › Display › Appearance** chooses light (the default), dark, or follow the device. Signed in, the choice is saved with your account and follows you to other devices.
