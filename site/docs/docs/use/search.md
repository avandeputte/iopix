# Search and filters

## The search box

Start typing and iopix suggests matches as you go: **people, tags, albums, folders, cameras, lenses, photographers, places, years and months**. Pick one and it becomes a **chip**. Add more chips to narrow down: "Emma" + "Beach" + "2019" shows photos of Emma at the beach in 2019.

Press **Enter** instead to search the words everywhere: file names, folders, titles, captions, tags, people, text recognised in the photos, and photo descriptions.

## Search by what is in the photo

With **content search** installed (see [Models](../ai/models.md)), choose **Photos that look like…** and describe what you want in plain words: "kids jumping into a lake", "snowy mountain at sunset", "birthday cake with candles". iopix finds photos by what they show, even when nobody tagged them.

From any photo, **More photos like this** does the same with the photo itself as the example.

## Filters

**Filters** opens a panel with every piece of metadata in the current results, each with a live count:

- type (photo or video), rating, favorites
- date range, years
- people, tags, albums and folders
- place, with or without a location
- camera, lens, ISO, aperture, focal length
- photographer, file type
- AI filters: sharpest, blurry, very dark, very bright, objects in the picture, people in the picture, best frame of each burst

Filters combine with the search chips.

## Smart albums

Any combination of search and filters can be kept: **Save search** in the bar above the grid. It appears under **Albums** as a smart album that updates itself as photos are added.
