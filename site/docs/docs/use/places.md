# Places and the map

## Places

**Places** sorts geotagged photos by **country, region and town**. The names are worked out on your server from each photo's GPS position, using the offline GeoNames data (about 30 MB, downloaded once). No photo positions are sent anywhere.

![Places](/img/places.jpg){.shot}

## The map

**Map** shows every geotagged photo in clusters that split as you zoom in. Click a cluster to see its photos.

![Map](/img/map.jpg){.shot}

- **Day's trail** draws the route of one day's photos.
- **Map / Satellite** switches the imagery, and is remembered.
- **Street View** is offered from a place's pin and in the info panel.

The map uses **OpenStreetMap** by default, with nothing to set up. You can switch to **Google Maps** with your own key: see [Google Maps and Cloud Vision](../integrations/google.md).

## Placing photos

Editors can put photos without a location on the map from **Map › Place photos**: find a place, pick photos from the tray, and click the map or drag them on. See [Dates and locations](../edit/dates-locations.md).
