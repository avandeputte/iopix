# Phones, tablets and apps

## In the browser

iopix adapts to any screen. On a phone:

- a **bottom tab bar** carries Photos, Explore, People and Search, with the rest under **More**,
- photos are **swiped** through,
- dialogs slide up as **sheets**,
- everything stays clear of the notch and the home indicator.

![iopix on a phone](/img/phone-explore.jpg){.shot style="max-width:260px"}

## Install it like an app

Over HTTPS, iopix installs as an app with its own icon and full screen:

- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** menu › **Install app**.
- **Desktop Chrome or Edge:** the install icon in the address bar.

On Android the installed app is also a **share target**: share photos to iopix and they open in Import. Photos and search always come from your server, so what you see is never out of date.

**Settings › Display › Name** sets what iopix calls itself: the top of the page, the browser tab and the installed app's name.

## Native apps for Apple devices

Native apps are in testing:

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **iPhone and iPad** | native Explore, viewer and photo screens, sharing, uploads from the photo library, and sign-in that keeps working as you move from Wi-Fi to cellular |
| **Mac** | the same app on the Mac, with a menu bar icon |
| **Apple TV** | slideshows, favorites, On this day, Then and now and random photos on the big screen |

They connect to your own iopix server; there is no account to create anywhere else.

On iPad and Mac the photo viewer keeps its **info pane** beside the photo, as in the browser, with the same Actions menu, section chooser and edit log. Its text can be selected and copied. **Edit** at the top of the pane (for accounts that can change photos) turns the pane into the editor, and **✕** goes back to the details. On iPhone the same pane opens as a sheet.

The viewer answers the browser's keys on a Mac or an iPad with a keyboard: ← → for the next and previous photo, 0 to 5 to rate, L for favorite, I for the info pane, F for the face boxes, E to edit, [ and ] to rotate, D to share, space for a slideshow, Delete to move to the trash and Esc to close. On the Mac the mouse wheel zooms towards the pointer; a trackpad pinch zooms and a two-finger scroll pans. Zooming in past the preview shows the original file. Face boxes can be clicked to name, confirm or adjust them.

The sidebar (More on iPhone) has **Library** for everyone: Favorites, Folders, Albums, Tags, Map and Events & trips. Each Library management screen that shows photos has a photo-size menu (List, Large, Larger), remembered on the device. Admins also get **Library management**: Import, Trash, Duplicate files, Near-duplicates, Bursts, Detected content, Metadata quality, Date suggestions and History.

The Map finds places, switches between map, satellite and hybrid, shows the photos of the visible area, draws a day's trail, and places photos that have no location. Places lists countries, their regions and towns. The Photos timeline groups by day or month and selects a whole day from its heading.
